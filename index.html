BEFORE YOU PRINT

Inside the ink.

Inspect the contract ↗
Who gets paid for an edition?

The four registered artists with the most visible tiles at mint time share four ink. With two artists, each receives two. The NFT records the split. Later patron payments use those same proportions, even if another player paints over the original tiles.

What happens when I scan a wall label?

You can view the print and its credits immediately. To send ink, create a free testnet player or use your existing one. The page shows the amount for each artist before you sign.

Does this cost real money?

The public studio runs on NEAR testnet. The faucet funds your dedicated account, and $INK is a testnet game token. Your browser stores the player key. Clearing site data removes access to that player.

What can someone verify independently?

The contract stores each edition’s original pixels and artist allocation. Standard NEAR token transfers record the payments. The public repository includes a live check for the deployed code hash and edition data.
